arXiv Enhancer

Download and rename arXiv papers with clean, descriptive filenames

Filename Template

Customize how your downloaded papers are named. Use the placeholders below to create your preferred format.

Available Placeholders:

{YYYY} Year of submission
{FirstAuthor} First author's last name
{Title} Paper title (sanitized)
{ID} arXiv paper ID

Preview:

Enter a template to see a preview

How to Use

  1. Navigate to any arXiv abstract page (e.g., arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
  2. Look for the "Download & Rename" button next to the PDF link
  3. Click the button to download the paper with your custom filename

Example

For the paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Ashish Vaswani et al. (2017):

  • Default template: {YYYY} - {FirstAuthor} - {Title}
  • Result: 2017 - Vaswani - Attention Is All You Need.pdf
